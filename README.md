### CallDeck 隐私政策

CallDeck（以下简称「本应用」）是一款纯本地的号码抽取与播报工具。我们高度重视你的隐私，本政策说明本应用如何处理（实际上是不处理）数据。

**1. 我们不收集任何数据**

- 无账号体系：无需注册、登录，我们不掌握任何用户身份信息
- 无埋点统计：不集成任何第三方分析 SDK，不记录使用行为
- 无广告、无内购、无订阅：本应用为付费买断制下载，不集成任何广告或支付 SDK
- 零系统权限：不申请通知、麦克风、相册、定位、蓝牙、通讯录等任何系统权限，运行时不会出现任何权限弹窗
- 语音播报说明：号码朗读使用 iOS 系统内置的本地语音合成引擎（AVSpeechSynthesizer），不联网、不录音、不经过任何第三方服务

**2. 付费说明**

- 本应用为 App Store **付费买断制**下载：一次购买，解锁全部功能，无任何内购、订阅或后续收费
- 购买与支付由 Apple 处理，本应用不接触你的支付信息；购买记录由 Apple 掌握，与本应用无关
- 退款请通过 Apple 官方渠道申请（reportaproblem.apple.com）

**3. 数据仅存储在你的设备上**

- 你的号码池设置、字母前缀、语音播报偏好、外观与语言选择，以及当前局的抽取记录，仅以本地文件形式保存在设备上
- 本应用不进行任何网络请求，任何数据都不会离开你的设备
- 卸载本应用即彻底、不可逆地删除全部本地数据

**4. 用途与合规**

- 本应用仅做随机号码的抽取、播报与展示，适用于聚会主持、课堂点名、团队破冰等通用场景
- 不涉及任何形式的投注、赌博、奖励或金钱交易

**5. 儿童隐私**

本应用不收集任何用户的任何数据，包括儿童。

**6. 政策变更**

若未来版本引入需要更新本政策的功能，我们将在本页面更新内容并标注新的日期。

## English

### CallDeck Privacy Policy

CallDeck (the "App") is a fully local number-draw and announcement tool. Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains how the App handles (i.e., does not handle) data.

**1. We collect no data**

- No accounts: no sign-up or login; we hold no identity information
- No analytics: no third-party analytics SDKs, no usage tracking
- No ads, no in-app purchases, no subscriptions: the App is a paid, one-time download with no ad or payment SDKs integrated
- Zero permissions: the App requests no system permissions (no notifications, microphone, photos, location, Bluetooth, or contacts) and shows no permission dialogs
- Voice announcements: number speech uses the iOS built-in, on-device text-to-speech engine (AVSpeechSynthesizer) — no network access, no recording, no third-party services

**2. Paid app**

- The App is a **paid, one-time purchase** on the App Store: pay once, unlock everything — no in-app purchases, no subscriptions, no future charges
- Purchases and payments are handled by Apple; the App never touches your payment information, and purchase records belong to Apple, not the App
- For refunds, use Apple's official channel (reportaproblem.apple.com)

**3. Data stays on your device**

- Your number pool settings, letter-prefix preference, voice preferences, appearance and language choices, and the current draw session are stored as local files on your device only
- The App makes no network requests; no data ever leaves your device
- Uninstalling the App permanently and irreversibly deletes all local data

**4. Purpose and compliance**

- The App only draws, announces and displays random numbers, for hosting party games, classroom picking and team ice-breakers
- It involves no form of betting, gambling, prizes, or monetary transactions

**5. Children's privacy**

The App collects no data from any user, including children.

**6. Changes to this policy**

If future versions introduce features requiring policy updates, we will update this page with a new date.
